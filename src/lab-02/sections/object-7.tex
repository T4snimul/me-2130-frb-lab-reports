\section{Object 7}
\markright{Object 7}

A cut-extrude formed the central bore of the gear blank, and a second cut/extrude formed a stepped recess on the face. This cut was mirrored across the part, and a circular pattern (33 instances) replicated it around the full 360° to generate the gear teeth.
